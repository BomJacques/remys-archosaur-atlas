# Phase 2 art style guide (read fully)

Goal: redraw every illustration in Remy's Archosaur Atlas in a polished, detailed "science-explainer" flat-vector
style: think Kurzgesagt, but with more detail. Reference piece: `phase2/chicken-test.svg`, generated by
`phase2/make_chicken.js`. Open the SVG in a browser and study it before drawing anything.

## Look
- Flat vector shapes, NO black outlines. Form comes from colour: 3 tones minimum per big shape (base, shadow, light),
  gradients allowed (linear/radial), soft blurred highlights (feGaussianBlur) used sparingly.
- Rim light: thin lighter stroke along the lit edge (top/front), low-opacity darker shadow shapes under belly/chin/tail.
- Detail that tells you what the animal IS: feather vanes/scallops, scales, skin folds, claws, teeth, nostril,
  eye with highlight, brow ridge, hands/feet with proper toes. Repeating texture (scale/feather fields) clipped
  to the body with `<clipPath>`; keep it ~60% opacity so it reads as texture, not noise.
- Bold readable silhouette first. At 120 px wide (home-page card) the animal must still be instantly recognisable.
- Colour: saturated but harmonious, with colour-shifted shadows (shadows lean blue/purple, highlights lean warm/yellow).
  Give each animal a distinct palette that matches its branch of the tree (see `DATA.taxa[].color` in js/data.js).
- Eyes: friendly, with a white highlight dot; predators get a slightly narrower brow, never scary/gory (audience is 8-12).
- Ground shadow: a soft dark ellipse under the feet (opacity ~.35). Animals face RIGHT. Marine/flying animals: no ground shadow.
- Science: follow the guard-rails in SPEC.md (feathers on maniraptorans and birds, T. rex hands not pronated and
  two-fingered, archosaur stance, correct hips, etc.). Where we are unsure (e.g. adult T. rex skin) pick the
  better-supported option and keep it plausible.

## Canvas + output contract
- Author on an **800 x 600** canvas (4:3), animal centred with ~40 px margin, **transparent background**
  (no backdrop circle; the chicken test's `<g id="backdrop">` is stripped by `wrap()` anyway).
- Write a node generator script per group in `phase2/gen/<group>.js` using `phase2/lib.js`:
  `L.wrap(artId, innerSvg, {label})` converts to the site format (viewBox 0 0 400 300, ids prefixed, safety checks),
  `L.writeFile(groupName, artObject)` writes `phase2/out/art2-<groupName>.js` (defines `window.ART[id] = '<svg…>'`).
  Run with `node phase2/gen/<group>.js`. Use plain ids (`id="body"`) inside your SVG; wrap() prefixes them.
- Reuse helpers from `lib.js` (`leafFeather`, `scaleField`, `rng`) and copy useful functions from `make_chicken.js`.
- **Size budget: <= 45 KB per SVG** (iPad-friendly). Prefer reusing `<defs>` gradients/`<use>` over duplicating geometry.
  Avoid heavy filters (use at most 2 blur filters per SVG; no feTurbulence).
- Forbidden: `<script>`, `on*=` attributes, external links/hrefs, `<image>`, fonts/`<text>` (the only exception is
  numbered markers in the skeleton comparison), width/height on the root.
- Group-level ids (e.g. `archosaur`, `theropod`) show a representative/ancestral-looking animal (they can include a
  faded "?" ghost silhouette for hypothetical ancestors), same quality bar as species.
- Each piece must be deterministic (use `L.rng(seed)` not Math.random).

## Process (mandatory)
1. Study the reference chicken SVG. Look at `DATA.taxa` (js/data.js) entries for your ids: nickname, diet, size, colour.
2. Draw ONE hero piece first, render it (see below), critique it against the reference, iterate until it matches the quality bar.
3. Then do the remaining pieces, reusing your tricks. Keep consistent palettes within a group.
4. Contact sheet: write a throwaway HTML page in the scratchpad dir
   `F:\Temp\claude\ec1e086069f15c18\385cf4b4-a6a8-4a35-97e2-93a9feef9417\scratchpad\<yourgroup>\sheet.html` that inlines all your SVGs in a grid
   (also render each at 120 px wide to check the silhouette). Open it via the preview server: http://localhost:8081/phase2/ is the
   project root server; to view scratch pages either copy the sheet into `phase2/sheets/<yourgroup>.html` (that folder is git-ignored)
   and open `http://localhost:8081/phase2/sheets/<yourgroup>.html` in the built-in browser (`mcp__Claude_Browser__*`, load with ToolSearch;
   use preview_start with a `url`, or navigate in an existing tab), then screenshot and LOOK at it. Iterate on every weak piece.
5. Verify: `node --check` on your generator; run it; confirm every id in your list exists in the output and passes the size budget.
6. Do not edit any file outside `phase2/gen/<yourgroup>*.js`, `phase2/out/art2-<yourgroup>.js`, and `phase2/sheets/<yourgroup>.html`.
   Do not run git. Do not touch js/ or css/.

## Report (brief)
Ids delivered, per-piece KB, which pieces are weakest and why, anything you'd redo with more time.
