# Family figures for the "Start here" page

Save each result as `F:\Claude code\drawings\family\<name>.png` (names below), then run
`python tools/art/import_family.py "F:/Claude code/drawings/family"`. The page swaps the grey placeholders for your
paintings automatically. "You" already uses the painted child from the atlas.

**Style block (use with every prompt):**
> Hand-illustrated natural-history field-guide style, like the painted child in the atlas (same rendering, same line weight, same colours). Realistic proportions, softly textured painterly shading, thin dark olive-brown outline, warm natural colours, friendly relaxed expression. ONE person only, full body standing, facing slightly to the right, simple everyday clothes, no accessories except where stated. Plain pure white background, no ground, no shadow, no text. Portrait orientation, about 3:4, high resolution.

Keep a family resemblance: warm brown skin, dark curly hair (greying on the grandparents), kind faces.
